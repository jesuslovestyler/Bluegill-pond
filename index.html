How to play

  1. Cast — tap the big button (or tap the water) to throw your line out.
  2. Watch the bobber — little twitches and dips are just nibbles. Don't bite on those!
  3. Set the hook! — when the bobber plunges under with a splash, tap fast. You've got about a second.
  4. Reel in — see what you caught. Most days it's bluegill… but the pond holds a few surprises.

Too early or too late and the fish is gone — that's fishing. Every catch counts toward your total, and the pond remembers your biggest of the day while you play.
